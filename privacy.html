1. Summary

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The game has no chat and no way to post or share content.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers for the game.
  • The game is free to play. You can optionally buy coins or an auto-renewable Premium subscription through Apple. Apple processes all payments; we never see your card or billing details.
  • To understand how the game is played and to measure our advertising, we plan to use a small number of partners (analytics, attribution and, if offered, optional rewarded ads). These partners are not yet integrated. Once they are, they receive the device and gameplay data described below.
  • We only access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow it in Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt (App Tracking Transparency). Saying no does not change the game.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Contract: processing purchases and delivering what you bought.
  • Consent: tracking with the IDFA (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt) and, where required, analytics and personalised ads. You can withdraw consent at any time (section 6); this does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Legitimate interests: non-personalised measurement of the game's performance, security and fraud prevention, provided your interests do not override ours.

5. Retention

  • On your device: until you delete the game.
  • Partners: where we control the setting, analytics and attribution data held by our partners is kept for up to 24 months and then deleted or aggregated. Aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be kept longer.
  • Purchases: Apple keeps purchase records under its own policy; we keep only what the partners above hold.

6. Your choices

  • Tracking: allow or deny tracking for the game in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you deny it, partners cannot access the IDFA.
  • Personalised ads (iOS): iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising controls Apple's own ads; partners' ads follow your App Tracking Transparency choice.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions.
  • Delete local data: delete the game from your device.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. Denying tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt (or in iOS Settings) opts you out of that sharing on this device.

We do not hold accounts, so we cannot look you up by name or email. To exercise a right, email insightops2@gmail.com; we will help you, including by forwarding a deletion request to our partners with the device identifiers they need. We answer within the period the law sets (usually one month under the GDPR, 45 days under the CCPA). We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country) and is not listed in the App Store Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact insightops2@gmail.com and we will help delete it. If ad partners are integrated, we will configure them not to treat users as child-directed, and to use non-personalised ads where a user is known to be under the applicable age.

9. International transfers

Our partners may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US / UK-US Data Privacy Framework, as described in each partner's policy.

10. Security

The game stores no passwords or payment details. Data sent to partners travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we choose partners that publish their security and privacy commitments.

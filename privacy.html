Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what data the Arcade Restore mobile game ("the game") and its partners collect, why, and the choices you have. The game is published by Ivan Vakulenko, an individual developer ("we", "us"). Contact: insightops2@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases. Ad, analytics and attribution partners collect device data as described below.
  • Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before personalized ads and analytics, and you can change your choice in Settings.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK)

We rely on your consent for personalized advertising and analytics, and on our legitimate interest for non-personalized ads, security and fraud prevention. You can withdraw consent at any time in the game's Settings; this does not affect processing that happened before.

4. Your choices

  • Consent: change it any time via Settings → Privacy in the game.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Remove Ads: an optional purchase that turns off non-rewarded ads.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Because we hold no account data, most requests are best sent to the partners below; we will help you with any request sent to insightops2@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Partners' privacy policies

  • AppLovin: applovin.com/privacy
  • GameAnalytics: gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Tenjin: tenjin.com/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

7. Retention

Partners keep data for the periods described in their policies. Game progress stays on your device until you delete it.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. International transfers

Our partners may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes will also be shown in the game.

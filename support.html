Support

Arcade Restore is made by Ivan Vakulenko, an individual developer. Questions, bug reports and privacy requests: insightops2@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version and the game version (shown in the App Store or Google Play) if you report a problem.

Restore purchases

The Premium subscription can be restored on a new device or after reinstalling the game:

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy.
  2. Open the game and go to the Shop.
  3. Tap Restore purchases.

On Android: sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you used to buy, open the game and go to the Shop; Premium and one-time items come back automatically (or tap Restore purchases).

Coins and other progress are stored only on your device and cannot be restored after the game is deleted.

Cancel the Premium subscription

Open iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions, choose Arcade Restore and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also do this in the App Store app: tap your profile, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next renewal. Premium stays on until the end of the paid period. Deleting the game does not cancel the subscription.

On Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, choose Arcade Restore and tap Cancel subscription. Premium stays on until the end of the paid period. Deleting the game does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds

All payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), so we cannot issue refunds ourselves. App Store: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: in the Google Play app, go to Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history and select the order, or see support.google.com/googleplay (Request a refund).

Delete your data

The game has no accounts and keeps your progress only on your device. To delete it, delete the game from your device. For data held by our partners, see the Privacy Policy or email insightops2@gmail.com.
